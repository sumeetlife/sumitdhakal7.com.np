Welcome

Welcome to My Personal Blog – Sumit Dhakal

Hello and welcome to my personal blog! I'm Sumit Dhakal, a management student, aspiring web developer, educator and technology enthusiast from Murgiya, Rupandehi, Nepal.

I believe that learning never stops. My interests connect business, technology, creativity and innovation. I enjoy exploring web development, digital platforms, online business, e-commerce, digital marketing and the ways technology is transforming the modern business environment.

Alongside my academic journey, I enjoy teaching, sharing knowledge and contributing to my community. Through this blog, I aim to document my experiences, share useful knowledge, express my ideas and connect with people who have similar interests.

This is more than just a website; it is a reflection of my learning journey, ambitions and the person I am continuously working to become.

Keep learning. Keep growing. Keep creating.
About Me

Who I Am

I am Sumit Dhakal, from Sainamaina-4, Murgiya, Rupandehi, Nepal. I have completed my higher secondary education in Management with Computer Science from Prabhat English Boarding Secondary School, achieving a GPA of 3.80.

Currently, I am pursuing BBA-F at Axis College, Butwal, affiliated with Pokhara University. My academic interests include business management, finance, information technology and the changing relationship between business and technology.

I am passionate about web development, digital business, e-commerce and creative problem-solving. I enjoy learning practical skills and applying them to real-world ideas.

Beyond academics and technology, I value social responsibility, teamwork and community involvement. I believe personal growth becomes more meaningful when we also contribute positively to the people around us.
